Privacy Policy

This page explains how CINEVA may handle information when you use our website.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CINEVA ("we", "our" or "the website") may collect, use and protect information relating to visitors and users. By using CINEVA, you acknowledge this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
  • Information you submit through support or contact channels.
  • VIP subscription and payment reference information.
  • Movie or series requests submitted through the website.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.
  • A random browser identifier stored in local storage to count unique daily visitors and understand general site traffic. This visitor counter does not collect IP addresses or account details.

The exact information collected depends on which website features and connected services are enabled.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain CINEVA.
  • Manage user accounts and access.
  • Process and verify VIP subscription payments.
  • Review movie and series requests.
  • Respond to support enquiries.
  • Protect the website against fraud, abuse and unauthorized access.

4. Payments

If you purchase a VIP subscription, payment processing may involve third-party payment providers. Payment details may be processed by those providers under their own privacy policies.

Never share your mobile money PIN, password or one-time verification code with CINEVA or anyone claiming to represent CINEVA.

5. Third-Party Services

CINEVA may rely on external services for hosting, database operations, authentication, payment processing or movie information. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect information. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your account or to request an appropriate correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as CINEVA changes. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through our Contact Us page.
