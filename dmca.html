Copyright Protection

DMCA & Copyright Policy

CINEVA respects the intellectual property rights of creators and copyright owners. This page explains how to report material you believe infringes your copyright.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Important: Please submit a notice only if you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the owner's behalf. Knowingly making false or misleading claims may have legal consequences.

1. Our Copyright Commitment

CINEVA respects the rights of copyright owners and expects users and contributors to do the same. We take copyright concerns seriously and will review complete, properly submitted notices concerning material made available through this website.

Where appropriate, we may restrict access to, remove, or disable access to material identified in a valid notice. We may also take action against repeat infringement where applicable.

2. How to Submit a Copyright Notice

If you believe content accessible through CINEVA infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to represent, send a notice through our Contact Us page. Include the subject line Copyright / DMCA Notice and provide all of the following information:

  1. Your full legal name and, if applicable, the name of the organization you represent.
  2. Your contact details, including an email address and a reliable way for us to reach you.
  3. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed. If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list.
  4. The exact URL(s) on CINEVA where the allegedly infringing material can be found. Please provide a separate URL for each item where possible.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury where applicable, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  7. Your physical or electronic signature.

Providing clear URLs and complete information helps us identify and review the material. Do not send passwords, payment PINs, or other sensitive account credentials.

3. Review and Response

After receiving a notice, CINEVA may review whether it contains enough information to identify the material and assess the claim. We may contact you for clarification or additional details. If appropriate, we may remove or disable access to the identified material while the matter is reviewed.

Submitting a notice does not guarantee a particular outcome or a specific response time. We may notify the person who provided or submitted the material when appropriate and legally permitted.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe material was removed or disabled because of a mistake or misidentification, you may contact us through the Contact Us page with the subject Copyright Counter-Notification.

Include:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and where it appeared before removal.
  • A statement, made in good faith, explaining why you believe the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
  • Any declarations or consent to jurisdiction required by the law that applies to your request.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

A counter-notification may be shared with the original claimant where legally required or appropriate. Restoration of material is not automatic and may depend on applicable law and the circumstances of the claim.

5. Third-Party Websites and Links

CINEVA may display links to websites or services operated by third parties. We do not control external websites or independently guarantee the availability or ownership status of material hosted by them. If your complaint concerns material hosted on another service, you may also need to contact that service or its hosting provider directly.

6. Repeat Infringement

Where applicable, CINEVA may limit or terminate access for users or contributors who repeatedly engage in copyright infringement. We may consider the reliability and completeness of notices, counter-notifications, and the surrounding circumstances when reviewing repeated complaints.

7. No Legal Advice

This page provides general information about CINEVA's copyright complaint process. It is not legal advice and does not create a legal relationship. Copyright rules and notice requirements vary by jurisdiction. Consider seeking independent legal advice if you are unsure about your rights or obligations.

8. Contact CINEVA

To submit a copyright notice, counter-notification, or question about this policy, use our contact page and clearly label your message so it can be routed for review.
